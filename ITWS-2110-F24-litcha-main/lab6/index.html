General Pokemon Information

Here you can find information regarding the currently displayed pokemon.

This includes its name, elemental types, pokedex number, height, weight, and abilities.

The elemental types result in certain advantages and weakness against other pokemon.

The pokedex number is the ID which pokemon are assigned to differentiate them from one another.

The abilities provide special effects in battle to pokemon.

Some abilities are activated when a certain condition is met, or when the pokemon first enters battle.
